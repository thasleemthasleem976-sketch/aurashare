Unified Send & Receive Flow

1
Click Send or Receive

No separate connect step. When you click Send, AuraShare auto-scans nearby peers. When you click Receive, it begins listening.

2
Choose Files & Tap Device

Pick files from your device, then simply click on the detected peer card. The transfer initiates instantly.

3
Receiver Authorizes & Saves

The receiver accepts the prompt, and the file streams directly over local TCP at up to 867 Mbps without internet data.
