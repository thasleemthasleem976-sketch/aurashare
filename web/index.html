How to Share Files in 4 Steps

1
Connect Devices Locally

Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi router, or turn on Mobile Hotspot on one device and connect the other. Internet is not required.

2
Discover Nearby Device

Open AuraShare on both devices. Click "Scan Nearby Devices" to let UDP multicast announce and locate the peer.

3
Select Files

In File Management, click "Browse Local Files" and pick documents, videos, photos, or archives.

4
Authorize & Transfer

The receiver sees an authorization pop-up. Once accepted, files stream over TCP directly at maximum hardware speed.
